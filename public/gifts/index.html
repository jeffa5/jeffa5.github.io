Gifts

For me

I do not expect gifts.

Given that, if you want to give me a present then I strongly prefer that you donate the value that you would have spent to my chosen charity: GiveWell’s All Grants Fund. No need for something to unwrap; either tell me directly about the donation or note it in a card. The donation is very much appreciated.

For others

I respect that everyone has their different preferences and ideas. Rather than gifting presents I pledge to donate a small amount (currently £10) to a charity of your choice. I have a preference for spending time with you and having real conversations with you, as a general catchup and to hopefully learn more about you and your chosen charity.

FAQ

Why donations over presents?

I think we all have enough material things in our lives and donating to charity can have a much more widespread impact.

Why GiveWell?

GiveWell is an organisation that compares the impact of various charities on multiple aspects and provides grants to the most impactful, browse their website for more information.
